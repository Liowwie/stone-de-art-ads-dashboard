Client Summary

In Q2 2026, Google Ads Spend rose 55.9% to RM 16,356 and Total Actual Conversions rose 45.8% to 516 (WhatsApp 478, Website 32, Walk-In 6). Unique Actual Paid Leads rose 63.4% to 250, so Actual Paid CPL improved from RM68.57 to RM65.43. In the showroom, the client recorded 50 visitors (Q1 2026: 24), 7 new customers and 10 deals.

Key figures
  • Google Ads Spend: RM 16,356 (Q1 2026: RM 10,491)
  • Clicks: 6,568 · CTR 6.91% · Avg. CPC RM2.49
  • Google Ads Conversions: 54 (platform count, not actual leads)
  • Total Actual Conversions: 516 = WhatsApp 478 + Website 32 + Walk-In 6
  • Paid / Organic / Unsure: 250 / 127 / 139
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM65.43 (Q1 2026: RM68.57)
  • Showroom Visitors: 50 (Paid 17 / Organic 18 / Unsure 15; Walk-In visits 4)
  • New Customers: 7 · Sales (Deals): 10
What improved
  • Actual Paid CPL improved 4.6% (RM68.57 → RM65.43)
  • Unique Actual Paid Leads rose 63.4% (153 → 250)
  • Total Actual Conversions rose 45.8% (354 → 516)
  • CTR rose from 6.44% to 6.91%
  • Google Ads Conversions rose (34 → 54)
  • Unsure-tagged share of leads fell from 34.5% to 26.9%
  • Showroom visitors rose (24 → 50)
What declined
  • Average CPC rose from RM1.87 to RM2.49
What caused the changes (where the data shows it)
  • By campaign, the largest change in Google Ads conversions was Collection (29 Oct 2025): 16 → 29, at RM 287 per conversion (was RM 382). Google Ads conversions are a platform signal and cannot be matched to individual WhatsApp leads.
  • Clicks rose 17.3%, mainly because impressions rose 9.3%; CTR improved by 0.47 points and average CPC rose 33.0%.
  • By channel: WhatsApp leads +149, website leads +12, Walk-In leads +1 versus Q1 2026.
  • Within the quarter, Jun 2026 had the highest cost per paid lead (RM75.64: RM 6,883 spend for 91 paid leads) and Apr 2026 the lowest (RM55.50). Quarter CPL is total spend ÷ total paid leads, not an average of the three months.
What we should optimise next quarter
  • Reinforce WhatsApp source-tagging at first contact.
  • Reduce or pause Quartzite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Kitchen Top & Island (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)) and Bathroom (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) — the highest cost per Google Ads conversion in their campaigns.
  • Review search terms and add negative keywords for Granite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), which spent RM 455 with no conversions in 01 Sept 2026 – 18 Oct 2026.
  • Test General (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) in a standalone campaign with its own budget for 3–4 weeks.
  • Protect the strongest ad groups: Feature Wall (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)), Marble (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Flooring (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) and Furniture (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)).
  • Make sure every showroom walk-in is also entered in the WhatsApp / Walk-In enquiry log, so it counts as a lead.
  • Ask the client whether deal values (RM) can be added to the Inquiry List sheet, so revenue and ROAS can be reported again.

Q2 2026 held efficiency broadly steady versus Q1 2026, and the showroom closed more business. The priority is to improve attribution quality while protecting the ad groups that are working.
