Client Summary

Q4 generated significantly more paid leads than Q3 2025, while the quarter's average Actual Paid CPL improved. However, performance became less efficient throughout the quarter. December was the strongest month, and by December, efficiency held roughly steady.

What Improved / What Did Not Improve

What improved
  • Actual Paid CPL improved 39.6% (RM 256 → RM 155)
  • Unique Actual Paid Leads grew 740.0% (5 → 42)
  • CTR rose from 3.68% to 6.45%
  • Confirmed Paid sales rose (0 → 2)
What did not improve
  • Unsure-attribution share rose from 36.4% to 72.7% of actual leads
5

What Changed During the Quarter

Spend moved from RM 1,282 to RM 6,502 (+407.1%). Unique Actual Paid Leads moved from 5 to 42 (+740.0%), so Actual Paid CPL improved 39.6%. Google Ads' own conversion count moved +29.6%, against +2100.0% for CSV-verified Total Actual Conversions — a meaningful divergence between the two measurement systems worth treating as a platform-reporting caveat, not a confirmed change in real demand.
