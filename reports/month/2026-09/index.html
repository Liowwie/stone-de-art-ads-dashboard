Client Summary

In September 2026, Google Ads Spend fell 7.4% to RM 5,166 and Total Actual Conversions fell 8.9% to 153 (WhatsApp 142, Website 11, Walk-In 0). Unique Actual Paid Leads fell 19.5% to 66, so Actual Paid CPL rose from RM68.04 to RM78.28. In the showroom, the client recorded 10 visitors (August 2026: 14), 4 new customers and 6 deals (August 2026: 2 and 5).

Key figures
  • Google Ads Spend: RM 5,166 (August 2026: RM 5,580)
  • Clicks: 2,056 · CTR 6.89% · Avg. CPC RM2.51
  • Google Ads Conversions: 16 (platform count, not actual leads)
  • Total Actual Conversions: 153 = WhatsApp 142 + Website 11 + Walk-In 0
  • Paid / Organic / Unsure: 66 / 33 / 54
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM78.28 (August 2026: RM68.04)
  • Showroom Visitors: 10 (Paid 3 / Organic 3 / Unsure 4; Walk-In visits 2)
  • New Customers: 4 · Sales (Deals): 6
What improved
  • New customers rose (2 → 4)
  • Deals closed rose (5 → 6)
What declined
  • Actual Paid CPL rose 15.0% (RM68.04 → RM78.28)
  • Unique Actual Paid Leads fell 19.5% (82 → 66)
  • Total Actual Conversions fell 8.9% (168 → 153)
  • CTR fell from 7.02% to 6.89%
  • Google Ads Conversions fell (19 → 16)
  • Unsure-tagged share of leads rose from 25.0% to 35.3%
  • Showroom visitors fell (14 → 10)
What caused the changes (where the data shows it)
  • Paid-tagged leads fell by 16 while Unsure-tagged leads rose by 12. Paid + Unsure together moved only -3.2% (124 → 120), so a large part of the paid-lead drop is likely leads that were not confirmed as Google Ads at first contact, rather than lost demand.
  • By campaign, the largest change in Google Ads conversions was SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026): 8 → 3.94, at RM 386 per conversion (was RM 205). Google Ads conversions are a platform signal and cannot be matched to individual WhatsApp leads.
  • Clicks fell 7.1%, mainly because impressions fell 5.2%; CTR slipped by 0.14 points and average CPC held steady.
  • By channel: WhatsApp leads -8, website leads -4, Walk-In leads -3 versus August 2026.
  • Showroom visitors fell (14 → 10) while deals rose (5 → 6). Deals and new customers are counted in the month they close, which is often weeks after the first enquiry or visit, so they reflect earlier months' leads as much as this period's.
What we should optimise next
  • Ask the team to confirm the source (Google Ads or not) for every new WhatsApp enquiry, and re-check this period's Unsure leads.
  • Reduce or pause Quartzite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Kitchen Top & Island (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)) and Bathroom (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) — the highest cost per Google Ads conversion in their campaigns.
  • Review search terms and add negative keywords for Granite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), which spent RM 455 with no conversions in 01 Sept 2026 – 18 Oct 2026.
  • Test General (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) in a standalone campaign with its own budget for 3–4 weeks.
  • Protect the strongest ad groups: Feature Wall (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)), Marble (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Flooring (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) and Furniture (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)).
  • Make sure every showroom walk-in is also entered in the WhatsApp / Walk-In enquiry log, so it counts as a lead.
  • Ask the client whether deal values (RM) can be added to the Inquiry List sheet, so revenue and ROAS can be reported again.

September 2026 cost more per paid lead than August 2026, although the showroom closed more business. Part of this is a tagging issue rather than a real loss of demand, so the priority is to fix lead-source tagging and trim the weakest ad groups before adding budget.
