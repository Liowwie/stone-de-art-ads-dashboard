Client Summary

January continued to generate strong enquiry volume, but efficiency held up against December 2025. Spend increased by almost 42%, while Unique Paid Leads increased by only around 97%. As a result, Actual Paid CPL improved from RM 95 to RM 68.

Key figures
  • Spend: RM 4,304
  • Unique Paid Leads: 63
  • Paid Submissions: 63
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM 68
  • Additional spend vs December 2025: approximately RM 1,276
  • Additional Unique Paid Leads vs December 2025: +31
What improved
  • Click volume increased
  • Total enquiry volume remained strong
  • Google Ads continued to generate demand
  • Actual Paid CPL improved (RM 95 → RM 68)
Main issues
  • A portion of WhatsApp leads remains classified as Unsure
  • Campaign-level WhatsApp attribution remains incomplete
  • No confirmed Google-attributed sales this period
Likely explanation

The extra January budget likely reached a broader or less ready audience. This is a likely interpretation rather than a confirmed cause because many WhatsApp leads cannot be matched reliably to individual campaigns.

What we should do next
  • Hold the total budget steady for 1–2 weeks
  • Reduce or pause only the weakest Ad Groups
  • Protect stronger themes such as Marble, General, Feature Wall
  • Review Search Terms and add Negative Keywords
  • Improve WhatsApp lead qualification
  • Improve showroom and sales tracking

January 2026 held efficiency steady while continuing to generate demand. The next priority is to keep monitoring CPL closely as spend scales.

What Happened

Spend moved from RM 3,028 to RM 4,304 (+42.2%). Unique Actual Paid Leads moved from 32 to 63 (+96.9%), so Actual Paid CPL improved 27.8%. Google Ads' own conversion count moved -52.9%, against +12.5% for CSV-verified Total Actual Conversions — a meaningful divergence between the two measurement systems worth treating as a platform-reporting caveat, not a confirmed change in real demand.
