Client Summary

December continued to generate strong enquiry volume, but efficiency held up against November 2025. Spend increased by almost 4%, while Unique Paid Leads increased by only around 256%. As a result, Actual Paid CPL improved from RM 322 to RM 95.

Key figures
  • Spend: RM 3,028
  • Unique Paid Leads: 32
  • Paid Submissions: 32
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM 95
  • Additional spend vs November 2025: approximately RM 126
  • Additional Unique Paid Leads vs November 2025: +23
What improved
  • Total enquiry volume remained strong
  • Google Ads continued to generate demand
  • Actual Paid CPL improved (RM 322 → RM 95)
Main issues
  • A portion of WhatsApp leads remains classified as Unsure
  • Campaign-level WhatsApp attribution remains incomplete
Likely explanation

The extra December budget likely reached a broader or less ready audience. This is a likely interpretation rather than a confirmed cause because many WhatsApp leads cannot be matched reliably to individual campaigns.

What we should do next
  • Hold the total budget steady for 1–2 weeks
  • Reduce or pause only the weakest Ad Groups
  • Protect stronger themes such as Marble, General, Feature Wall
  • Review Search Terms and add Negative Keywords
  • Improve WhatsApp lead qualification
  • Improve showroom and sales tracking

December 2025 held efficiency steady while continuing to generate demand. The next priority is to keep monitoring CPL closely as spend scales.

What Happened

Spend moved from RM 2,902 to RM 3,028 (+4.3%). Unique Actual Paid Leads moved from 9 to 32 (+255.6%), so Actual Paid CPL improved 70.7%. Google Ads' own conversion count moved -5.6%, against +21.9% for CSV-verified Total Actual Conversions — a meaningful divergence between the two measurement systems worth treating as a platform-reporting caveat, not a confirmed change in real demand.
