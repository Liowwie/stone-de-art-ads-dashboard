Client Summary

October continued to generate lower enquiry volume, but the additional budget was less efficient than in September 2025. Spend fell by about 55%, while Unique Paid Leads fell by around 80%. As a result, Actual Paid CPL increased from RM 256 to RM 573.

Key figures
  • Spend: RM 573
  • Unique Paid Leads: 1
  • Paid Submissions: 1
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM 573
  • Additional spend vs September 2025: down RM 710
  • Additional Unique Paid Leads vs September 2025: -4
What improved
  • Google Ads continued to generate demand
Main issues
  • Spend increased much faster than paid lead growth (-55.3% vs -80.0%)
  • Actual Paid CPL worsened (RM 256 → RM 573)
  • A portion of WhatsApp leads remains classified as Unsure
  • Campaign-level WhatsApp attribution remains incomplete
  • No confirmed Google-attributed sales this period
Likely explanation

The extra October budget likely reached a broader or less ready audience. This is a likely interpretation rather than a confirmed cause because many WhatsApp leads cannot be matched reliably to individual campaigns.

What we should do next
  • Hold the total budget steady for 1–2 weeks
  • Reduce or pause only the weakest Ad Groups
  • Protect stronger themes such as Marble, General, Feature Wall
  • Review Search Terms and add Negative Keywords
  • Improve WhatsApp lead qualification
  • Improve showroom and sales tracking

October 2025 was not a failure, but the additional budget did not produce enough additional paid leads. The next priority is to improve efficiency before increasing spend again.
