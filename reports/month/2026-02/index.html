Client Summary

February continued to generate lower enquiry volume, but the additional budget was less efficient than in January 2026. Spend fell by about 27%, while Unique Paid Leads fell by around 41%. As a result, Actual Paid CPL increased from RM 68 to RM 84.

Key figures
  • Spend: RM 3,126
  • Unique Paid Leads: 37
  • Paid Submissions: 37
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM 84
  • Additional spend vs January 2026: down RM 1,178
  • Additional Unique Paid Leads vs January 2026: -26
What improved
  • Google Ads continued to generate demand
Main issues
  • Spend increased much faster than paid lead growth (-27.4% vs -41.3%)
  • Actual Paid CPL worsened (RM 68 → RM 84)
  • Google Ads platform conversions increased faster than real recorded paid leads
  • A portion of WhatsApp leads remains classified as Unsure
  • Campaign-level WhatsApp attribution remains incomplete
  • No confirmed Google-attributed sales this period
Likely explanation

The extra February budget likely reached a broader or less ready audience. This is a likely interpretation rather than a confirmed cause because many WhatsApp leads cannot be matched reliably to individual campaigns.

What we should do next
  • Hold the total budget steady for 1–2 weeks
  • Reduce or pause only the weakest Ad Groups
  • Protect stronger themes such as Marble, General, Feature Wall
  • Review Search Terms and add Negative Keywords
  • Improve WhatsApp lead qualification
  • Improve showroom and sales tracking

February 2026 was not a failure, but the additional budget did not produce enough additional paid leads. The next priority is to improve efficiency before increasing spend again.

What Happened

Spend moved from RM 4,304 to RM 3,126 (-27.4%). Unique Actual Paid Leads moved from 63 to 37 (-41.3%), so Actual Paid CPL worsened 23.7%. Google Ads' own conversion count moved +25.0%, against -48.6% for CSV-verified Total Actual Conversions — a meaningful divergence between the two measurement systems worth treating as a platform-reporting caveat, not a confirmed change in real demand.
