Client Summary

In February 2026, Google Ads Spend fell 27.4% to RM 3,126 and Total Actual Conversions fell 48.3% to 75 (WhatsApp 68, Website 6, Walk-In 1). Unique Actual Paid Leads fell 41.3% to 37, so Actual Paid CPL rose from RM68.32 to RM84.50. In the showroom, the client recorded 4 visitors (January 2026: 11).

Key figures
  • Google Ads Spend: RM 3,126 (January 2026: RM 4,304)
  • Clicks: 1,616 · CTR 6.08% · Avg. CPC RM1.93
  • Google Ads Conversions: 10 (platform count, not actual leads)
  • Total Actual Conversions: 75 = WhatsApp 68 + Website 6 + Walk-In 1
  • Paid / Organic / Unsure: 37 / 16 / 22
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM84.50 (January 2026: RM68.32)
  • Showroom Visitors: 4 (Paid 0 / Organic 3 / Unsure 1; Walk-In visits 1)
  • New Customers: N/A · Sales (Deals): N/A
What improved
  • Google Ads Conversions rose (8 → 10)
  • Unsure-tagged share of leads fell from 39.3% to 29.3%
What declined
  • Actual Paid CPL rose 23.7% (RM68.32 → RM84.50)
  • Unique Actual Paid Leads fell 41.3% (63 → 37)
  • Total Actual Conversions fell 48.3% (145 → 75)
  • CTR fell from 6.41% to 6.08%
  • Average CPC rose from RM1.81 to RM1.93
  • Showroom visitors fell (11 → 4)
What caused the changes (where the data shows it)
  • By campaign, the largest change in Google Ads conversions was Signature Works (06 Jan 2025): 2 → 8, at RM 178 per conversion (was RM 708). Google Ads conversions are a platform signal and cannot be matched to individual WhatsApp leads.
  • Clicks fell 32.1%, mainly because impressions fell 28.4%; CTR slipped by 0.33 points and average CPC rose 6.9%.
  • By channel: WhatsApp leads -71, website leads +1, Walk-In leads 0 versus January 2026.
What we should optimise next
  • Hold spend and move budget away from the weakest ad groups before increasing it again.
  • Reduce or pause Quartzite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Kitchen Top & Island (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)) and Bathroom (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) — the highest cost per Google Ads conversion in their campaigns.
  • Review search terms and add negative keywords for Feature Wall (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)), Granite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) and Travertine (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), which spent RM 1,682 with no conversions in 01 Sept 2026 – 07 Oct 2026.
  • Test General (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) in a standalone campaign with its own budget for 3–4 weeks.
  • Protect the strongest ad groups: Marble (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Flooring (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) and Furniture (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)).
  • Reduce Unsure tagging (29% of leads) by confirming each new WhatsApp enquiry's source at first contact.
  • Ask the client whether deal values (RM) can be added to the Inquiry List sheet, so revenue and ROAS can be reported again.

February 2026 cost more per paid lead than January 2026. The priority is to restore lead efficiency and trim the weakest ad groups before adding budget.
