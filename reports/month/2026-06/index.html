Client Summary

In June 2026, Google Ads Spend rose 29.6% to RM 6,883 and Total Actual Conversions rose 7.6% to 184 (WhatsApp 166, Website 16, Walk-In 2). Unique Actual Paid Leads rose 8.3% to 91, so Actual Paid CPL rose from RM63.22 to RM75.64. In the showroom, the client recorded 13 visitors (May 2026: 17), 2 new customers and 4 deals (May 2026: 2 and 3).

Key figures
  • Google Ads Spend: RM 6,883 (May 2026: RM 5,311)
  • Clicks: 2,440 · CTR 6.73% · Avg. CPC RM2.82
  • Google Ads Conversions: 27 (platform count, not actual leads)
  • Total Actual Conversions: 184 = WhatsApp 166 + Website 16 + Walk-In 2
  • Paid / Organic / Unsure: 91 / 48 / 45
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM75.64 (May 2026: RM63.22)
  • Showroom Visitors: 13 (Paid 3 / Organic 5 / Unsure 5; Walk-In visits 1)
  • New Customers: 2 · Sales (Deals): 4
What improved
  • Unique Actual Paid Leads rose 8.3% (84 → 91)
  • Total Actual Conversions rose 7.6% (171 → 184)
  • Google Ads Conversions rose (17 → 27)
  • Deals closed rose (3 → 4)
What declined
  • Actual Paid CPL rose 19.6% (RM63.22 → RM75.64)
  • CTR fell from 7.04% to 6.73%
  • Average CPC rose from RM2.52 to RM2.82
  • Showroom visitors fell (17 → 13)
What caused the changes (where the data shows it)
  • By campaign, the largest change in Google Ads conversions was Signature Works (06 Jan 2025): 2 → 15, at RM 228 per conversion (was RM 1,235). Google Ads conversions are a platform signal and cannot be matched to individual WhatsApp leads.
  • Clicks rose 15.7%, mainly because impressions rose 21.0%; CTR slipped by 0.31 points and average CPC rose 12.0%.
  • By channel: WhatsApp leads +8, website leads +6, Walk-In leads -1 versus May 2026.
  • Showroom visitors fell (17 → 13) while deals rose (3 → 4). Deals and new customers are counted in the month they close, which is often weeks after the first enquiry or visit, so they reflect earlier months' leads as much as this period's.
What we should optimise next
  • Hold spend and move budget away from the weakest ad groups before increasing it again.
  • Reduce or pause Quartzite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Kitchen Top & Island (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)) and Bathroom (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) — the highest cost per Google Ads conversion in their campaigns.
  • Review search terms and add negative keywords for Feature Wall (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)), Granite (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) and Travertine (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), which spent RM 1,682 with no conversions in 01 Sept 2026 – 07 Oct 2026.
  • Test General (Collection (29 Oct 2025)) in a standalone campaign with its own budget for 3–4 weeks.
  • Protect the strongest ad groups: Marble (Collection (29 Oct 2025)), Flooring (Signature Works (06 Jan 2025)) and Furniture (SW_Furniture & KTI (22 Jul 2026)).
  • Make sure every showroom walk-in is also entered in the WhatsApp / Walk-In enquiry log, so it counts as a lead.
  • Ask the client whether deal values (RM) can be added to the Inquiry List sheet, so revenue and ROAS can be reported again.

June 2026 cost more per paid lead than May 2026, although the showroom closed more business. The priority is to restore lead efficiency and trim the weakest ad groups before adding budget.
