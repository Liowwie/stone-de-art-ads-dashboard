Client Summary

May continued to generate strong enquiry volume, but the additional budget was less efficient than in April 2026. Spend increased by almost 28%, while Unique Paid Leads increased by only around 12%. As a result, Actual Paid CPL increased from RM 56 to RM 63.

Key figures
  • Spend: RM 5,311
  • Unique Paid Leads: 84
  • Paid Submissions: 84
  • Actual Paid CPL: RM 63
  • Additional spend vs April 2026: approximately RM 1,148
  • Additional Unique Paid Leads vs April 2026: +9
What improved
  • Click volume increased
  • Total enquiry volume remained strong
  • Google Ads continued to generate demand
Main issues
  • Spend increased much faster than paid lead growth (+27.6% vs +12.0%)
  • Actual Paid CPL worsened (RM 56 → RM 63)
  • Google Ads platform conversions increased faster than real recorded paid leads
  • A portion of WhatsApp leads remains classified as Unsure
  • Campaign-level WhatsApp attribution remains incomplete
  • No confirmed Google-attributed sales this period
Likely explanation

The extra May budget likely reached a broader or less ready audience. This is a likely interpretation rather than a confirmed cause because many WhatsApp leads cannot be matched reliably to individual campaigns.

What we should do next
  • Hold the total budget steady for 1–2 weeks
  • Reduce or pause only the weakest Ad Groups
  • Protect stronger themes such as Marble, Feature Wall, Granite, Flooring
  • Review Search Terms and add Negative Keywords
  • Improve WhatsApp lead qualification
  • Improve showroom and sales tracking

May 2026 was not a failure, but the additional budget did not produce enough additional paid leads. The next priority is to improve efficiency before increasing spend again.

What Happened

Spend moved from RM 4,163 to RM 5,311 (+27.6%). Unique Actual Paid Leads moved from 75 to 84 (+12.0%), so Actual Paid CPL worsened 13.9%. Google Ads' own conversion count moved +70.0%, against +5.0% for CSV-verified Total Actual Conversions — a meaningful divergence between the two measurement systems worth treating as a platform-reporting caveat, not a confirmed change in real demand.
