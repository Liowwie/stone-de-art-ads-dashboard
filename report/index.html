How to read the numbers in this report
Google Ads Conversions
A count Google's own platform reports (e.g. clicks it thinks led to a form fill or call). It is measured differently from — and, in this account, is consistently lower than — the leads actually recorded in the website and WhatsApp logs. Useful for platform-side bid optimisation, not for judging real lead volume.
Actual Paid Leads
Real website + WhatsApp submissions, cleaned of test/spam/duplicates, that are explicitly tagged "Paid" (i.e. attributed to Google Ads). This is the number Actual Paid CPL is built from.
Total Actual Leads
All cleaned website + WhatsApp leads regardless of source tag — Paid, Organic, Unsure, and Unspecified combined. Shows total demand, not just what's attributable to ads.
Organic Leads
Actual leads explicitly tagged as arriving without paid ad influence (e.g. direct search, referral, word of mouth).
Unsure / Unspecified Leads
Actual leads where the source could not be confidently tagged Paid or Organic at the time of entry. These are kept out of both the Paid and Organic counts — folding them into either would overstate that channel's performance.
Sales
Confirmed closed deals from the sales log, tagged Paid / Organic / Unsure by the same convention as leads. Only "Paid" sales are used for ROAS.

Why Actual Paid CPL is the headline efficiency metric: Google Ads Conversions is a platform-side estimate that this account's own numbers show diverging from real recorded contacts. Actual Paid CPL (spend ÷ Actual Paid Leads) is grounded in real, deduplicated records from the business's own lead logs, so it is the more trustworthy signal for judging whether ad spend is translating into real enquiries.

Data-quality limitations

  • Sales attribution is a manual tag with no order ID/click ID/UTM link — only 11 sales rows exist in total, 2 of which are confirmed Google-attributed.
  • Website-lead UTM campaign tags (e.g. “collection”, “signature_work”) map to a campaign family, not an exact Google Ads campaign — multiple dated campaigns share the same tag.
  • WhatsApp leads carry no campaign-level attribution field at all.
  • RSA export contains no actual headline/description creative text — only ad-group-level performance metrics are available.
  • Quality Score is present on only a small fraction of keywords; no fabricated account-wide average is shown.
  • 874 rows in the WhatsApp export were blank template rows with no date, name, or phone number and were excluded entirely.

What this means for the business

Are ads generating more or fewer real leads? More — Actual Paid Leads grew +63.4% quarter-on-quarter (153 → 250).

Is lead efficiency improving or declining? Improving — Actual Paid CPL moved -4.6% (RM 69 → RM 65).

Which lead source is driving results? WhatsApp, by a wide margin (93.7% of Q2 2026 actual leads vs 6.3% from the website form).

Which campaign needs attention? Signature Works — its cost per conversion (RM321.91) is running above Collection (RM286.50) despite a similar budget share.

Is revenue attribution reliable enough to judge ROAS? Not yet. Only 2 confirmed Google-attributed sale(s) exist across the whole data range, both from December 2025 — there is no confirmed sale to check against the leads generated in Q1 or Q2 2026.

What should the client approve or change next? Approve continuing the current spend and budget split; do not approve a further spend increase based on ROAS until an updated, better-attributed sales log is provided.
