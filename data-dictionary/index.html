Actual Paid CPL
Derived
—Google Ads Spend ÷ Unique Actual Paid Leads—Denominator is unique Paid leads across all three sources (WhatsApp + Website + Walk-In). Deliberately excludes Organic and Unsure leads, and uses the deduplicated count, not raw Paid Submissions.
Showroom Visits
stone-de-art-whatsapp-leads.csv
Inquiry Type = "Walk-In"Count of unique Walk-In rows—The same Walk-In rows that feed Walk-In Conversions — this is the later funnel stage (someone who visited the showroom), not a separate or double count. Undercounts overall showroom traffic: it cannot capture visits that originated from a website lead. See Showroom Visitors for the client's fuller log.

stone-de-art-google-sales.csv
Organic / Paid, SalesCount / Sum of rows tagged "Paid"—Only a small number of confirmed Paid sales exist in the dataset — see Attribution Confidence on the dashboard for the current count. Organic and Unsure sales shown separately, never included here.
Google Ads / Platform ROAS
Derived
—Confirmed Paid Revenue ÷ Google Ads Spend—Attribution confidence shown alongside — treat as directional given the small sample.
Total Marketing Spend
stone-de-art-client-metrics.csv (transcribed from the client's own reference report)
Total Marketing SpendSum over range, only months with a reported figure—NOT the same figure as "Spend"/"Google Ads Spend" elsewhere on this dashboard. This is the client's own blended cost — a flat SEO retainer + Google Ads Search spend (SEM) + a flat "Unsure/unattributed" line, inclusive of 8% withholding tax per the client's latest reference report — used only for the 4 Client Business metrics below, matching how the client's own sheet computes their ROAS/CPV/CPA. Only reported for Jan 2026 onward.
Sales Revenue
stone-de-art-google-sales.csv
SalesSum of every recorded sale in range, regardless of Paid/Organic/Unsure tag—Broader than "Confirmed Paid Revenue" (which counts only Paid-tagged sales, used for Platform ROAS). This is the client's whole-business revenue figure, used for Client Sales ROAS.
Client Sales ROAS
Derived
—Sales Revenue ÷ Total Marketing Spend—Shown as N/A when there is no confirmed revenue in the period, or no Total Marketing Spend figure available — never shown as a literal 0.00x. Distinct from Google Ads / Platform ROAS (which uses only Paid-tagged sales ÷ Google Ads CSV spend).
Showroom Visitors
stone-de-art-client-metrics.csv (transcribed from the client's own reference report)
Showroom VisitorsSum over range, only months with a reported figure—The client's own dedicated showroom visitor log — a more complete, authoritative count than the dashboard's separate "Showroom Visits" figure (which is only a WhatsApp "Walk-In" tag proxy and can't see visits that started as a website enquiry). Only reported from Oct 2025 onward.
Cost Per Visit (CPV)
Derived
—Total Marketing Spend ÷ Showroom Visitors—Refers to actual showroom visits, not website visits. N/A when either figure is unavailable, or Showroom Visitors is 0.
New Customers
stone-de-art-client-metrics.csv (transcribed from the client's own reference report)
New CustomersSum over range, only months with a reported figure—The client's own count of genuinely new client relationships (deduplicated by the client, not derived from Google Ads conversions or raw sale count — a repeat customer's 2nd sale doesn't add a New Customer). Only reported from Jan 2026 onward.
Cost Per New Customer
Derived
—Total Marketing Spend ÷ New Customers—The client's business-level CPA — not the same as "Cost per Google Ads Conversion". N/A when New Customers is 0 or unavailable.
Ads Cost % of Revenue
Derived
—Total Marketing Spend ÷ Sales Revenue × 100—Lower is better (less revenue spent on advertising). N/A when Sales Revenue is 0 or unavailable, rather than dividing by zero.
